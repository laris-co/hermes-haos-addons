broker opening

Opening the broker. Each board that describes itself will be printed here as its own view of the air.

The picture is the reading. Haze is printed as flat courses rising from the horizon: one thin course is clean air, six drowned courses are hazardous air. Bands, ranges and colours follow the US EPA PM2.5 scale, in µg/m³ — the number itself is stamped small, like a seal, in the corner of each print.
Four blocks, no gradients. sumi, ai, mizu, beni on cream paper; every other tone here is one block overprinted on another.
A board that has not reported is left un-inked. No number is invented for a silent sensor, and a zero is never printed in place of one. Press s for broker settings.
