Saying hello to the air boards.

The six weather-friends

Colours and cut-offs follow the US EPA PM2.5 scale, measured in µg/m³ (millionths of a gram of smoke dust in a cubic metre of air).

Every friend is drawn from the board itself. A card appears when a sensor announces itself over Home Assistant discovery, and the name on it is the board id taken from the topic it publishes on — not the sensor’s label, which is “PM2.5” on every one of them.
A sleeping friend is telling the truth. When a board is not sending, its friend closes its eyes and shows no number at all. An empty card or a hopeful zero would be a lie about the air in that room.
Press s to change the broker, or Esc to close it again.

Broker settings

This wall talks MQTT over a WebSocket. Port 1884 is the WebSocket listener; the plain MQTT port cannot be opened by a browser at all, and trying looks exactly like a wrong passphrase.
