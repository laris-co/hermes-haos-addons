About this sheet

Twenty presentations, one engine. Each version reuses the MQTT layer, discovery parsing, availability and expiry handling and EPA banding from tools/mqtt-dashboard.html, and replaces only what you see. All twenty are single self-contained files whose one external dependency is unpkg.com/mqtt@5.15.2. The glass set adds six local JPEGs in img/ — the only local assets in the folder.

The previews load lazily, on purpose. Twenty walls opening twenty MQTT connections the moment this page loads would be twenty sessions on the lab broker for a page nobody has scrolled yet. Every iframe carries loading="lazy", so a wall connects only when its thumbnail comes into view. Scroll to the bottom and all twenty exist; open the page and walk away and only the first row does.

† Seven of versions 11–20 name their lineage in the file itself. Three — Shout, Module and Journal — describe a method rather than a person; the attribution here is this sheet’s reading of that method, not a claim the files make.

‡ Versions 21–30 name an interface grammar, not a designer: Control Center, the Lock Screen, the Dynamic Island, visionOS depth. They are studies in a shared visual language rather than homages to an individual, and none of them claims any affiliation.

The six photographs are generated images, not photographs of a specific real place or moment. They illustrate what each EPA band looks like from a valley rim; they are not evidence, and nothing on any wall presents them as a record of a particular day.

Four bugs shipped once in the original wall and are designed out of every version here: CSS uppercasing turning µg/m³ into mg/m³, a reading sized in viewport units splitting 163.3 across two lines, all three boards sharing the discovery name "PM2.5", and wildcard subscriptions. README.md beside this file has the full account.

Demo mode is opt-in from the URL and marked everywhere it is on. The band bench at the top writes ?demo= into this page’s address bar and hands it to the one function that composes every preview’s src, and nowhere else — no stored flag, no default, no survival across a reload without the parameter. The previews ship with no src at all, so a wall is never fetched once without the flag and then re-pointed with it. While it is set, the sheet grows a sticky hazard-taped bar naming the exact value being simulated, every thumbnail is tabbed DEMO, and the tab title is prefixed. A wall in demo mode opens no broker connection at all, because these are health displays for children: one screen must never be able to carry a real reading and an invented one at the same time. Fake clean air on a wall is how a child is sent outside on a hazardous day.

A thumbnail is never blank when something is wrong. A wall that cannot reach the broker, or that the broker turns away, replaces its whole screen with one calm sentence saying what to do — so twenty identical sentences on this sheet means the broker wants a login, not that the sheet is broken. Reopen it with ?u=&p=, use the sign-in above, or open one wall and press s.
