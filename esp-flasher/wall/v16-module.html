R06 · C01–C12 · NOTES & PROVENANCE

WHAT IS DRAWN. One module per station, built from a retained Home Assistant discovery message. A station that has not published has a hatched slot, never a zero. Every discovery payload here is named “PM2.5”, so the identity printed on each module is the board id read out of its own state topic.

WHAT IS NOT DRAWN. No averaging, no smoothing, no last-known value dressed up as current. A reading older than its expiry is struck as expired and its mark is removed from the rule. A negative concentration is a fault, not a good reading.

ASSUMED UNITS. The PM2.5 unit is taken from the discovery payload verbatim. Units on the auxiliary channel table are inferred from the channel name and are not published by the board — treat them as a label, not as evidence.
